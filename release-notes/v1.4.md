## DuoPlayer Hi-Fi 1.4

- 17 languages, chosen automatically from the system language / 界面支持 17 种语言，自动跟随系统语言
- New app icon: a play button split into A (gold) and B (blue) / 新图标：一个播放键分成 A（金）和 B（蓝）
- Lyrics: Japanese kana and Korean lines are now recognized as the CJK line / 歌词：日文假名、韩文也能识别为中日韩那一行
- Lyrics screen buttons size to their text / 全屏歌词顶部按钮按文字自适应宽度
- Error messages from the audio engine are translated too / 音频引擎的错误提示也已翻译
