## DuoPlayer Hi-Fi 1.5

- The player is now the first thing you see: A / B bars and the Play button are at the top, settings below, output status at the bottom / 打开即是播放区：A/B 播放条和播放键在最上面，设置卡片在下面，输出状态放到最底部
- Tap an empty A / B bar to choose audio right away / 点空的 A/B 播放条可以直接选择音频
- Output warnings stay at the top so they are not missed / 「系统只有 48kHz」等警告仍显示在最上方
